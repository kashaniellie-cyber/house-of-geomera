About Ellie The Intelligence Brief Private Office

Ellie Kashani is the founder of House of GeoMera, a private real estate advisory created for individuals, families, investors, and family offices seeking a trusted advisor for significant property decisions.

With more than fifteen years of experience across New York, London, and Dubai, Ellie has advised clients on the acquisition, disposition, and strategic management of exceptional real estate. Her role extends far beyond identifying properties. She represents her clients' interests from the earliest stages of the process through every aspect of execution, ensuring each decision is approached with discretion, clarity, and a long-term perspective.

House of GeoMera was established on the belief that exceptional real estate requires exceptional coordination. Rather than relying on multiple disconnected advisors, clients gain access to a carefully structured ecosystem where every critical discipline works together seamlessly. Legal counsel, private banking, financing, tax specialists, architects, interior designers, relocation professionals, immigration experts, insurance advisors, and other trusted specialists are brought together under one coordinated framework, allowing every stage of the journey to be managed with continuity, confidentiality, and precision.

Every professional within the House of GeoMera ecosystem has been selected for their expertise, experience, and reputation in their respective field. This integrated approach provides clients with a self-sufficient advisory platform where complex matters are coordinated through a single trusted relationship, ensuring the highest standards of professionalism while preserving the privacy and discretion that sophisticated transactions demand.

Whether acquiring a primary residence, establishing an international property portfolio, relocating across borders, or navigating a complex acquisition, Ellie serves as a long-term advisor dedicated to protecting her clients' interests, simplifying complexity, and delivering thoughtful solutions tailored to their individual objectives.

The most important real estate decisions begin the moment a property search starts. Ownership structure, financing strategy, legal and tax considerations, succession planning, and assembling the right advisory team all influence the success of an acquisition. Ellie believes that informed decisions at the outset create stronger outcomes. Through the House of GeoMera ecosystem, clients gain early access to the knowledge, strategic guidance, and exceptional professionals required to move forward with confidence, discretion, and a clear long-term vision.
